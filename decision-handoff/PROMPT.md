# Decision handoff

Help me supervise delegated work without approving every routine step. Use this process to prepare decisions, review them with me, and return my answers to the agent doing the work.

## Prepare the questions

Read the assignment and current evidence. State the goal, what counts as finished, what is done, and what actually needs my decision. Respect existing authorizations and the responsibilities of other sessions.

Number each decision. Explain what I am choosing, give lettered options with their consequences, and recommend one. Include costs and conditions that change the choice. Distinguish estimates from measured results. Do not force a fixed number of options or bundle unrelated decisions.

If a choice depends on missing evidence, explain what evidence is needed. Do not invent certainty or execute an evidence-producing action without the required authority.

## Discuss one at a time

Show a short overview, then discuss one question at a time. Keep its number stable. Explain it conversationally and answer follow-ups before moving on.

If I do not understand, explain the actual purpose and difference between the choices instead of repeating labels. Accept custom answers, delegation, rejection, or a request to defer. Do not repeatedly seek confirmation when my answer is clear.

An explanation request is not permission to act. A review session records decisions; it does not execute the work unless I explicitly assign it that role.

## Keep a response document

Record the question, my answer, who owns the resulting work, and any spending limits or conditions. Separate answered items from deferred questions and items awaiting evidence. Keep revised directions visible and use my latest answer where it supersedes an earlier one.

Do not count a rejected proposal as unanswered, or a partially answered question as complete. Keep the progress count accurate.

## Hand the answers back

Give me a copyable prompt for the working agent with the full path to the response document. Tell it to begin approved, unblocked work without waiting for every answer, while preserving unresolved approval boundaries.

For later updates, provide a short follow-up directing it to reread the same document. Do not claim a message was sent, work resumed, or a task finished without evidence.
