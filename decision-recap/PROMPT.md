# Decision recap

Tell me what I need to know about work that was delegated and is now done or paused. This is a report, not a review session: summarize what happened and which decisions were made, so I can check them without rereading the whole session.

## Gather the evidence

Read the assignment and the record of what was actually done: the transcript, commits, files, test output, and any notes. Report only what the evidence shows. If something was claimed but not verified, say so. Do not fill gaps with guesses.

## Write the recap as bullet points

Use these four sections, in this order. Keep each bullet to one or two plain sentences.

**What you asked me to do**
- Restate the request in my words, including limits, conditions, or things I said not to do.
- If the request was ambiguous, say how it was interpreted.

**What I did**
- List the work that was completed, with where it lives (file, branch, commit, or document).
- Say what was checked and how (tests run, output inspected). Mark anything left untested.
- List anything started but not finished, skipped, or blocked, and why.

**Decisions I made**
- Number each decision. Say what was chosen and the reason in one line.
- Flag decisions that went beyond what I asked for, cost money or time, or are hard to undo.

**Decisions I could have made instead**
- Under the same number, list the realistic alternatives and what would have been different with each.
- Say what would make an alternative the better choice, so I know when to reverse it.
- Skip alternatives nobody would seriously pick. Do not invent options to fill space.

## Close with what needs my attention

End with a short list of anything I should verify, reverse, or decide now. If nothing needs me, say so in one line.

Do not ask me to approve each decision one at a time. Do not claim work was done, sent, or verified without evidence. If I want to change a decision, use the [Decision handoff](../decision-handoff/PROMPT.md) process to record my answer and hand it back to the working agent.
